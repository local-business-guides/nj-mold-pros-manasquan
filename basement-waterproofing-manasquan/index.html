Start with what you’re seeing

For basement waterproofing Manasquan homeowners should begin by describing the signs, not guessing at a fix. Water on the floor, recurring dampness, or a musty area can each point to a different concern. Note where you first noticed it, whether it returns, and what parts of the basement are affected. That context helps a professional assess the situation instead of treating one visible spot as the whole problem.

A quick checklist before arranging an assessment:
- Mark the areas where you’ve seen water or dampness.
- Write down when it happens and whether it has happened before.
- Move belongings away from affected spots if it’s safe to do so.
