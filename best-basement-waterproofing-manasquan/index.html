A hiring checklist for your project

Before you make a decision, use these questions to compare proposals and clarify expectations:

  • What conditions at my property led to this recommendation?
  • Which locations or parts of the basement are included?
  • Is the work intended to address water entry, water damage, or both?
  • What should I move or make accessible before work starts?
  • What concerns should I bring up if I notice a change later?

Don’t treat a broad promise as a substitute for understanding the job. Avoid assuming that a product or repair used elsewhere will suit your basement. Also, don’t leave out earlier water issues just because the area looks dry at the moment. A complete description helps the contractor respond to the actual concern.
