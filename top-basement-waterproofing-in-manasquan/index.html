What the work may involve

Waterproofing service is intended to address water entry and moisture concerns. The specific work depends on what’s happening at the property, so ask what the proposed solution is meant to do and what parts of the basement it covers. If you’re also dealing with damage from water, ask whether water damage restoration should be discussed as part of the project.

A short checklist can help you prepare for that conversation:

  • Point out each area where you’ve noticed water or dampness.
  • Share any changes you’ve observed over time.
  • Ask what the recommended work addresses, and what it does not.
  • Confirm what parts of the basement need to be accessible.
  • Ask how you’ll know what work has been completed.

Avoid choosing a repair based only on one visible spot, or assuming that the same answer fits every basement. Share what you’ve observed and ask for an explanation before work begins.
